India generates massive volumes of electronic waste annually, yet 95% of e-waste flows into the toxic informal sector due to the absence of a centralized, intelligent discovery platform.

This leads to:

Unsafe recycling practices

Severe environmental pollution

Worker health hazards

Loss of $62+ billion worth of recoverable raw materials

Lack of public awareness and traceability

There is an urgent need for a centralized, AI-powered, trust-based digital platform that connects waste generators (citizens, businesses, institutions) with certified, government-authorized e-waste recycling centers.
